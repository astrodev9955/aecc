### What it is

Construction assumes specified copper, steel, and concrete actually enter the building. Over-order, damage, and offcuts mean up to 8% of material value is lost before handover — and the as-built carbon record is lost with it. Circular captures the supplier invoice at delivery, logs the purchase, and attaches indicative A1–A3 carbon and secondary-market salvage value. Ten years later, the owner hands a buyer a birth certificate instead of paying for a forensic survey.

### Key strengths

- Capture step is a photo of a receipt — no BIM plugin required
- Live ledger of spend, kgCO₂e, salvage, and recoverability
- Printable building passport for sale, refinance, or LCA handoff
- Copper / metals salvage made visible, not only carbon
- Honest about limits: indicative ICE factors, not a certified LCA

### Common use cases

- Jobsite delivery log → material passport
- Owner diligence file for sale or refinance
- Contractor waste / over-order visibility
- Pre-LCA inventory for sustainability consultants
- Circularity / salvage valuation at end of life

### Limitations to know

- Carbon factors are industry averages until EPDs are attached
- Purchase-based inventory is not a whole-building LCA
- Demo OCR is library-assisted; production will use a dedicated document model
- Does not replace waste-haul tickets or as-built BIM
